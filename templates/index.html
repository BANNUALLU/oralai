Features

Everything for Oral Health Screening

From image capture to PDF report — OralAI handles the complete AI screening pipeline.

🤖

Claude AI Analysis

Claude Vision observes all 3 dental images and writes a detailed personalised analysis with findings and urgency level.

🎯

YOLO Detection

YOLOv8 detects caries, stains, malalignment and pit fissure caries with bounding boxes and confidence scores.

📄

PDF Report

Professional multi-page PDF with YOLO images, treatment recommendations and AI analysis in the patient's language.

💬

WhatsApp Delivery

Send the PDF report directly to the patient's WhatsApp number instantly after screening is complete.

🌍

9 Languages

Full support for English, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Bengali and Arabic.

🛡️

Image Validation

Claude validates each image before processing — rejects non-teeth photos automatically.

Process

How OralAI Works

Simple 4-step process from patient details to report delivery.

📋

Enter Details

Patient fills name, age, gender, phone, email and preferred language.

📸

Capture Images

3 guided captures — Upper, Front and Lower teeth with real-time AI validation.

⚡

AI Analysis

YOLO detects conditions. Claude analyses all 3 images and writes a detailed diagnosis.

📤

Get Report

Download PDF, receive via Email or WhatsApp in patient's chosen language.
