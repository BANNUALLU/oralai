Section 02

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to provide OralAI's screening service:

  • To capture and validate 4 images — your face photo first, then 3 dental images
  • To run YOLOv8 object detection on your dental images
  • To generate a personalised AI analysis report in your chosen language
  • To create and deliver your PDF screening report via download, email, or WhatsApp
  • To improve the accuracy and performance of our AI models (anonymised only)
  • To respond to support requests or contact inquiries
We do not use your data for advertising, marketing to third parties, or selling to any external organisation.
🔒
Section 03

Data Storage & Security

OralAI is hosted on AWS EC2 (Amazon Web Services) infrastructure with the following protections:

  • All data transmitted over HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • Server access restricted to the development team only
  • Face and dental images are processed in real-time and not permanently stored on our servers after the report is generated
  • Patient details are stored securely and not exposed publicly
All 4 images (face + 3 dental) are used only during the active screening session and are not retained after your report is generated.
🦷
Section 04

Image & Health Data

Because OralAI processes dental and face images (a category of health-related data), we take extra precautions:

  • All 4 images are processed only by our AI pipeline (YOLOv8 + AI language model)
  • Your face image is used for patient identification context in the PDF report only — it is not used for facial recognition
  • No human team member manually reviews your images unless you contact us for support
  • Images are not shared with dentists, clinics, or any third party without your consent
  • AI-generated reports are for informational purposes only and do not constitute a medical diagnosis
The OralAI report does not replace a professional dental examination. Please consult a licensed dentist for clinical decisions.
📱
Section 05

WhatsApp & Email Delivery

When you choose to receive your report via WhatsApp or Email:

  • Your phone number is used only to send the PDF report via the WhatsApp Business API
  • Your email address is used only to deliver the screening report
  • We do not send promotional messages, newsletters, or unsolicited communications
  • Your contact details are not shared with WhatsApp/Meta beyond what is required for message delivery
🤝
Section 06

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share data only in limited situations:

  • Service providers: AWS (hosting), WhatsApp Business API, email service — solely to operate OralAI
  • Legal obligation: If required by Indian law, court order, or government authority
  • Academic research: Only fully anonymised, aggregated data — never individual records
No third-party advertiser, insurance company, employer, or organisation ever receives your personal data or images.
⚖️
Section 07

Your Rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 (India), you have the right to:

  • 📄 Access — Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • ✏️ Correct — Request correction of inaccurate personal information
  • 🗑️ Delete — Request deletion of your personal data from our systems
  • ❌ Withdraw Consent — Withdraw consent to data processing at any time
  • 📩 Grievance — Lodge a complaint with our Data Protection Officer

To exercise any right, contact us at oralai.site@gmail.com with subject: "Data Request – [Your Name]". We will respond within 72 hours.
