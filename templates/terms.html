Section 02

Description of Service

OralAI provides an AI-powered oral health screening service that includes:

  • Guided capture of 4 images — 1 face photo followed by 3 dental images (upper, front, lower teeth)
  • YOLOv8-based detection of possible dental conditions from the submitted dental images
  • AI-generated written analysis in multiple Indian and global languages
  • Downloadable PDF screening reports
  • Optional report delivery via WhatsApp or email

OralAI is developed as a final year academic project by students of Swarnandhra College of Engineering and Technology (Autonomous), Andhra Pradesh, India, under the guidance of Dr. Bomma Rama Krishna (HOD – AIDS & AIML).

As an academic project, OralAI is provided free of charge with no commercial intent. The service may be updated or discontinued as part of ongoing academic development.
