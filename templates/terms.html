Section 02

Description of Service

OralAI provides an AI-powered oral health screening service that includes:

  • YOLOv8-based detection of possible dental conditions from user-submitted images
  • AI-generated written analysis of dental images in multiple Indian and global languages
  • Downloadable PDF screening reports
  • Optional report delivery via WhatsApp or email

OralAI is developed as a final year academic project by students of Swarnandhra College of Engineering and Technology (Autonomous), Andhra Pradesh, India.
